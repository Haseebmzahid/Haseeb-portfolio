## Creative direction
An engineer's observatory: a precisely lit aluminium laptop above an engraved orbital platform. Oversized, left-aligned editorial typography establishes identity. A small technical index and project controls frame the object. Three projects become three different product worlds: warm architectural real estate, pale blue analytical valuation and dark financial charts.

About is a cinematic contact sheet: all three authentic supplied images are layered at different depths, with selectable cuts, slow image movement and scroll response. Experience brings that photographic identity into a metallic constellation. Face remains front-facing and undistorted. A complete HTML timeline is always available; mobile uses the contact sheet and timeline without orbiting labels.

## Design principles and typography
Graphite #0b0d10, warm white #eeede8, silver #9a9fa8, restrained ice #c4dcec. Fine rules replace glowing card borders. Geist is retained for its readable technical voice; a Georgia italic editorial accent softens the headline. Geist Mono appears only in indexes and metadata. Compare the rendered font specimen during QA; no additional display font network request is warranted. Layout alternates cinematic object, broad horizontal case studies, photographic editorial, typographic skill ledger, constellation and open contact workspace.

## Motion and interactions
One entrance per scene. Pointer rotation is damped and bounded; scroll shifts the laptop slightly without obscuring its screen. Project selection swaps the texture attached to the display geometry. Orbit labels travel actual XYZ paths, pause on hover/focus/selection and open the corresponding timeline record. Portrait cuts crossfade; faces never spin. A global pause control freezes ambient movement. Reduced motion freezes all ambient scenes and removes smooth scrolling. Native anchors and buttons are keyboard accessible.

## Implementation and performance
Next App Router and existing backend remain. Dynamically imported R3F scenes, procedural laptop with keyboard/trackpad/hinge/bezel, locally generated SVG display textures, studio lights and local environment. Use instanced keyboard geometry, DPR 1–1.5, no postprocessing, no remote HDRI and lazy offscreen scene mounting. Demand rendering except while visible ambient motion is enabled: visible continuous orbit and damping require frames. Stop frames offscreen, on pause and for reduced motion. R3F owns and disposes geometry/materials; cached textures are shared. Mobile replaces WebGL with original screen assets and a readable timeline. No GSAP or alternate scroll framework.
